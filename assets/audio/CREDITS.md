The bundled WAV files are edited excerpts of public preview recordings (Freesound) and an original WAV (BigSoundBank). Changes include mono conversion, precise trimming, light filtering, gain staging, fades, and crossfaded room and crowd loops. `tools/prepare_audio.py` records every source URL, excerpt, and processing step. Processed clips remain CC0.

Ball sounds come from separate moments in a real indoor volleyball game, retaining the actual hand slap, ball skin, nearby shoes, and gym reflection. Spikes, serves, blocks, passes, and sets randomly select related recordings and play as one clean contact rather than stacked, pitched copies. A quiet real-gym room loop sits behind the action. Regular AI footsteps remain selective so the ball stays at the front of the mix; the wordless serve crowd remains below the court.
